# Tree-navigation source history

The current `tree-navigate.ts` was independently authored from a behavioral
specification and public Pi SDK documentation/type declarations. Its author did
not inspect Tau or the preceding project implementation. The repository's
`provenance/tree-navigation.md` records those inputs; this notice does not claim
legal clearance.

https://github.com/milanglacier/pi-tau-web-server revision
